# Review and prompting

Check visible evidence:
- Focal identity: can the creature be described in one memorable sentence?
- Silhouette: do family and stage survive removal of texture and color?
- Small-size readability: review at intended game size; 96-128px is a useful initial proxy, not a rule.
- Family continuity: retain a few clear anatomical/color anchors.
- Evolution: change temperament, proportions, center of gravity, anatomy, and organ function.
- Detail hierarchy: keep calm regions and a clear focal area.
- Anatomy and animation: inspect joints, attachment points, occlusion, and motion feasibility.
- Originality: assess animal, colors, organ combination, silhouette, and posture together; recoloring is insufficient.

Report: ID -> visible issue -> specific change -> approved invariants.
Never claim an unperformed size or silhouette test passed.

Prompt scaffold:
Asset: original collectible creature review board.
Layout: families, stages, readable IDs, safe padding.
Rendering: chosen medium; clear contours, broad colors, restrained shadows.
Concept: animal + one leading integrated feature + personality.
Family anchors: a few inherited shapes/colors.
Juvenile: proportions, expression, capability.
Adolescent: structural and behavioral change.
Mature: functional payoff, silhouette, temperament.
Palette and detail budget: based on target display and production.
Keep: approved invariants.
Change: exact review corrections.
Avoid: competing ornaments, noisy materials, same-face enlargement, reference anatomy leakage.

For original designs, prefer textual abstraction of design principles over direct character-image imitation.
For identity continuation, use the approved creature itself as reference.
Keep unresolved review findings visible; generation does not imply acceptance.
