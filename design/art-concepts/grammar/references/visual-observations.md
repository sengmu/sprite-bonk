SpriteBonk lessons:
- Early designs overloaded leaves, strands, scales, molten cracks, and particles.
- Simplified designs reduced noise but still risked same-face enlargement.
- Grass: flower, leaf ears, wood horns, vines, and moss competed for attention.
- Fire: gills, back plates, horns, tail flame, chest glow, and flaming wings repeated the same attribute.
- Water: grass-calf references leaked mammal anatomy and fur.
- Previous calf/salamander/turtle forms are provisional, not permanently approved.
