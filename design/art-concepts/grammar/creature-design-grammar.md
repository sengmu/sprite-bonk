# Original Collectible Creature Design Grammar

Design memorable original creatures suitable for their actual game display size. Learn abstract principles from reference art instead of reproducing a recognizable existing character combination.

## Design principles
1. Define animal, ONE leading memorable body feature, and personality in one sentence. Keep secondary features only when they reinforce that idea.
2. Solve head/body/limb/tail proportions and black silhouette before surface detail. Do not hide weak structure behind fur, scales, glow, or particles.
3. Organize anatomy with broad color blocks. Start with main, secondary, and small accent colors when useful; this is not a hard color limit.
4. Integrate elemental expression into ears, neck, horns, shell, limbs, or tail. Avoid a normal animal carrying many disconnected elemental ornaments.
5. Express personality through eye shape, mouth, posture, and center of gravity. Do not give all species or all stages the same cute face.
6. Preserve a few family anchors while changing proportions, function, posture, and temperament across evolution. Enlargement plus added accessories is insufficient. Final forms need not become humanoid, winged, or muscular.
7. Establish silhouette, then a focal organ, then restrained pattern. Complexity depends on screen size and role; detailed designs can work when hierarchy remains clear.

## Workflow
- Research or review requests do not authorize generation.
- Resolve animal, primary feature, personality, family anchors, stage changes, and intended use from context.
- For SpriteBonk, deliver an overall numbered review board first; revise issues and obtain design acceptance before splitting final production assets. Other projects follow their own agreement.
- Prefer clear contours, broad colors, and restrained shading during review. Use the user's chosen medium; 2D and 3D are both valid.
- When asked to simplify, remove competing structures and redundant elemental expressions, not just texture.
- When asked for distinctiveness, strengthen one central idea rather than adding decorations.
- Assign image references explicit roles. Do not use one strong anatomical reference for unrelated species; it can leak mammal faces, hooves, or fur into reptiles.
- Respect chosen image tooling. Explicit GPT image generation uses built-in image_gen and imagegen skill. Anima/ComfyUI routes through comfyui-animatool. This skill itself authorizes neither bulk production nor tool changes.
- Review using references/review-and-prompts.md. Cite visible problems and concrete minimal corrections.
- Distinguish review boards, concepts, transparent portraits, sprites, animation, and models. A board is not a completed production asset suite.
